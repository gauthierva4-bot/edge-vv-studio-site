Privacy Policy

Effective October 22, 2026

This policy explains how EDGE Verse & Vision Studio ("we") handles information in EDGE CG SOFTWARE (the "App").

Information we collect

  • Account information: name, email address, business name and business details you provide.
  • Content you upload: photos, videos, logos and text about your business.
  • Connected platform data: when you connect Instagram, Facebook, Threads, Pinterest, LinkedIn or Google Business Profile via OAuth, we receive access tokens and the page/profile information, posts, comments, messages and insights those platforms make available under the permissions you grant.
  • Business analytics: if you connect Square, Shopify, Clover, Toast or Google Business Profile, we receive read-only sales, order and performance data.
  • Billing: payments are processed by Stripe. We do not store full card numbers.
  • Basic usage and device data needed to run and secure the App.

OAuth tokens

Access tokens are stored encrypted in our database (hosted on Supabase), used only to perform the actions you authorize (publishing posts, replying to comments and messages, reading insights and analytics), and deleted when you disconnect an account or delete your account. You can also revoke access at any time in each platform's settings.

How we use platform data

We use data only to provide the App to you: creating and scheduling content, publishing to your own accounts, replying on your behalf, reviewing content quality, and producing analytics and weekly recaps. We use AI service providers and Descript to generate text and video; they process data only to provide those services to us. We comply with the Meta Platform Terms, Pinterest, LinkedIn and Google API policies, including Google API Services User Data Policy limited-use requirements.

No sale of data

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information or platform data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Sharing

We share data only with service providers needed to run the App (such as Supabase, Stripe, Descript and AI providers), with the platforms you connect when you tell us to publish, or when required by law.

Retention

We keep data while your account is active and delete it within 30 days after account deletion, except where the law requires us to keep records (for example billing records).

Your rights

You can access, correct or delete your data by emailing edgevvstudio@gmail.com.

Data deletion instructions

To delete your data from EDGE CG SOFTWARE:

  1. Email edgevvstudio@gmail.com with the subject "Data Deletion Request" from the email on your account, and include your business name.
  2. We will confirm and delete your account, uploaded content, stored OAuth tokens and connected platform data within 30 days.
  3. To remove the App's access on Facebook or Instagram yourself, go to Settings & Privacy > Settings > Apps and Websites, find EDGE CG SOFTWARE and click Remove. You can do the same in the connected-apps settings of Threads, Pinterest, LinkedIn and Google.

Children

The App is for businesses and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy and will post the new date here.
